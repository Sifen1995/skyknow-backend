# Transferring airtime to another number

A prepaid customer can send part of their airtime balance to another prepaid number.

## Steps
1. Choose Transfer airtime in the app or dial the transfer menu.
2. Enter the receiver's number and the amount.
3. Confirm with the PIN. Both numbers receive an SMS.

## Limits
There is a minimum and a maximum amount per transfer and a daily limit. Transfers cannot be reversed once confirmed, so the number must be checked carefully.
