# Home broadband installation

New fixed broadband customers need a site visit before service starts.

## Process
1. Take the application with a valid ID and a delivery address.
2. Check that the area has fibre or wireless coverage.
3. Book a technician visit.

## Timing
Installation normally takes one to three working days after the application is approved. The customer must be at home for the visit. The router is provided by the technician.
