# Changing the tariff plan

Customers can move from their current tariff to a cheaper or larger plan.

## Steps
1. Check the customer's current plan and monthly usage.
2. Explain the options and the price difference.
3. Confirm the change. It starts at the next billing cycle unless the customer asks for immediate change.

## Notes
Some plans have a minimum contract period. Unused balance on the old plan does not always carry over.
