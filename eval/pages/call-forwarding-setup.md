# Call forwarding setup

Call forwarding sends incoming calls to another number, for example when the phone is off or busy.

## Steps
1. Open the phone's call settings and choose Call forwarding.
2. Choose when to forward: always, when busy, when unanswered or when unreachable.
3. Enter the number to forward to and save.

## Notes
Forwarded calls are charged to the person who set up the forwarding. To switch it off, return to the same menu and choose Disable.
