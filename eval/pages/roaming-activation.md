# International roaming

Customers who travel abroad must have roaming enabled before leaving, and enough balance or a roaming package.

## Steps
1. Check that the SIM is registered and the account is in good standing.
2. Enable international roaming for the number.
3. Advise the customer to buy a roaming package, because standard rates abroad are high.

## Notes
Roaming does not work in every country. Data roaming is off by default on many phones and must be turned on in the phone settings. Enabling roaming can take up to a few hours.
