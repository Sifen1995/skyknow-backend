# SMS not received

Customers sometimes say they are not receiving text messages, including one-time codes.

## Checks
1. Is the phone memory full or is the message app blocking unknown senders?
2. Does the number receive other messages? Ask a friend to send a test message.
3. Is the SIM in a roaming area or out of signal?

## Fix
Restart the phone and re-register on the network. If one-time codes from banks or apps are missing, check whether the sender number is on a block list. Escalate if no message arrives for 24 hours.
