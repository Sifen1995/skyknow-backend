# Voicemail setup

Voicemail records messages when the customer cannot answer.

## Steps
1. Enable voicemail in the phone's call settings or by calling the voicemail service from the phone.
2. Record a greeting and set a voicemail PIN.
3. Test by calling the number from another phone and leaving a message.

## Notes
Messages are stored for a limited number of days. Listening to voicemail while abroad may cost extra.
