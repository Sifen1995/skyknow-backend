# telebirr PIN reset

A customer who forgot the telebirr PIN, the secret code used to approve payments, can reset it.

## Requirements
The customer must be the registered owner of the wallet and must have the SIM in their phone.

## Steps
1. Open the telebirr app and choose Forgot PIN.
2. Enter the registered phone number and the one-time code received by SMS.
3. Choose a new 4-digit PIN.
If the SIM was replaced recently, the reset can take longer and the customer may need to visit a shop.
