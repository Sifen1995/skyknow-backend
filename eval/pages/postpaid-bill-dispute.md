# Postpaid bill dispute

A postpaid customer questions a charge on their monthly invoice.

## Steps
1. Verify the account holder.
2. Open the itemised bill and find the disputed charge.
3. Explain the charge if it is correct, such as roaming, premium SMS or a package renewal.
4. If it looks wrong, open a billing dispute and give the customer the case number.

## Notes
The undisputed part of the bill must still be paid on time. Disputes are normally answered within a set number of working days.
