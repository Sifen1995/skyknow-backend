# SIM swap and replacement

A SIM replacement gives the customer a new SIM card when the old one is lost, stolen, damaged or no longer works. The customer keeps the same phone number.

## Requirements
The customer must visit a service shop with a valid government-issued ID. The agent confirms that the ID matches the registered owner of the number. The old SIM is blocked as soon as the new one is activated.

## Steps
1. Confirm the customer's identity with the ID and a one-time code sent to an alternative number.
2. Block the old SIM.
3. Issue the new SIM and register it to the same number.
4. Ask the customer to restart the phone and test a call.
