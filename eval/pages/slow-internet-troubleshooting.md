# Slow internet troubleshooting

Use these steps when a mobile data customer says the internet is slow.

## Checks
1. Is the data package finished or throttled after a fair-use limit?
2. Is the signal weak? Ask the customer to move near a window or outside.
3. Is a large app update or video running in the background?
4. Restart the phone and switch airplane mode on and off.

## If it continues
Record the location and time, and open a network quality ticket. Widespread slowness in one area is handled by the network team, not by the customer.
