# Lost or stolen phone: block the SIM

When a phone is lost or stolen, the customer should protect their number, mobile money wallet and personal data.

## Steps
1. Verify the customer and block the SIM at once to stop calls and data.
2. Lock the telebirr wallet so that no payment can be made.
3. Record the phone's IMEI number if the customer has it, so the handset can be blocked.
4. Arrange a SIM replacement so the customer keeps the same number.

## Notes
Advise the customer to report the theft to the police and keep the case number.
