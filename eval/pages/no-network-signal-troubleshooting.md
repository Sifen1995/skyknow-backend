# No network signal

A customer reports no network, no bars, or the message Emergency calls only.

## Steps
1. Check that the SIM is active and not blocked or suspended.
2. Ask the customer to remove and re-insert the SIM, then restart the phone.
3. Turn automatic network selection on and try the SIM in another phone.
4. If the SIM works in another phone, the first phone is faulty. If not, replace the SIM.

## Area outage
If other customers nearby also have no signal, check the outage list and give the expected repair time.
