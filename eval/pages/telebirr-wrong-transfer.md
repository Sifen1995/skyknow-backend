# telebirr wrong transfer

Use this page when a customer sent money to the wrong telebirr number by mistake.

## Steps
1. Ask the customer for the transaction ID, the amount, the time and the wrong number.
2. Check that the transaction is completed and not pending.
3. Open a reversal request and give the customer the case number.
4. Tell the customer that the receiver must agree to return the money, or the case goes to the disputes team.

## Notes
Money is never taken back from a receiver without their agreement or a decision by the disputes team. The customer should not send the money again until the case is closed.
